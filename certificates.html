Branch Conversation & Operations Checklist

01 · Understand the need

Ask about amount, investment horizon, preferred payout frequency, need for liquidity, and whether the customer may need financing against the certificate.

02 · Confirm eligibility

Verify customer identity, legal capacity, account status, product eligibility, ownership/beneficiary rules and required documents using current bank procedures.

03 · Confirm product details

Check live product status, minimum amount and multiples, term, rate type, payout frequency, accrual start, maturity date, renewal and early redemption rules.

04 · Explain return clearly

Distinguish fixed, variable and step-down rates. Explain that a variable rate can change and a step-down rate changes by year.

05 · Redemption request

Identify the certificate, select full/partial redemption, enter the amount when partial, choose the receiving account, review settlement and applicable charges, then follow the authorized confirmation workflow.

06 · Finance against certificate

Check current eligible percentage, pricing, tenor, repayment method, fees, customer affordability, and approval authority. Never infer an approved limit from this simulator.

Important Service Workflow Reference

NBE's published digital-service guide describes redemption by selecting the certificate, choosing partial or full redemption, entering the partial amount if applicable, selecting the receiving account, and accepting the terms/settlement before confirmation. The screen in this tool is a training/reference aid only.

Before executing any operation, use the live NBE system and the latest approved circulars. This app does not connect to customer accounts or the bank core system.
