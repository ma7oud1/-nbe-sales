Customer Handling & Operations Guide

01 · Understand Customer Needs

Ask about investment amount, term, preferred payout frequency, liquidity needs, and possible finance needs against the certificate.

02 · Check Eligibility

Verify identity, legal eligibility, account status, product conditions, ownership and beneficiary rules, and required documents.

03 · Review Certificate Details

Check availability, minimum investment and multiples, term, rate type, payout frequency, accrual start, maturity, renewal, and early redemption rules.

04 · Explain Returns

Explain fixed, variable, and step-down returns. Variable rates may change; step-down rates differ by year.

05 · Redemption Request

Select the certificate and full or partial redemption, enter the partial amount if applicable, choose the transfer account, review settlement and fees, and follow approved confirmation steps.

06 · Finance Against Certificate

Review finance margin, pricing, tenor, repayment method, fees, affordability, and approval authority. This calculator is not an approved credit limit.

Important Service Reference

The bank’s published digital services guide describes redemption as selecting the certificate, choosing full or partial redemption, entering the amount for partial redemption, selecting the transfer account, and reviewing terms and settlement before confirmation. This page is a training and reference tool only.

Use the live bank system and latest approved instructions before any transaction. This tool is not connected to customer accounts or the core banking system.
